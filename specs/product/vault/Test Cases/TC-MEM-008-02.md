## Preconditions

- A Member holds two Roles, A and B

## Steps

1. Remove Role A from the Member, leaving only Role B
2. Attempt an action requiring only Role B's permission
3. Attempt an action requiring only Role A's (now-removed) permission

## Expected result

The Role-B-only action succeeds; the Role-A-only action is now blocked.
