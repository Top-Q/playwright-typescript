## Preconditions

- Target email has no existing account

## Steps

1. Click 'Add member'
2. Enter an external email address
3. Select a Role
4. Click Add

## Expected result

User appears in the Members list with Status 'Invited'; the invited user has no Project access until they complete registration/activation.
