## Preconditions

- A Board with at least two lists is open

## Steps

1. Drag a card to a new list
2. Measure time to visual update
3. Measure time to server-confirmed persistence

## Expected result

UI reflects the new state within 300ms (optimistic update); the change is confirmed persisted server-side within 2 seconds.
