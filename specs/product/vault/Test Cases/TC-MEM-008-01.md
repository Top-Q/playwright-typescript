## Preconditions

- A Member holds two Roles, each granting a distinct permission not shared by the other

## Steps

1. Have the Member perform an action requiring the first Role's permission, then an action requiring the second Role's permission

## Expected result

Both actions succeed, demonstrating union rather than intersection semantics.
