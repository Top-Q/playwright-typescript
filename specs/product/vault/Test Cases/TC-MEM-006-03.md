## Preconditions

- The Project has exactly one Member holding a manage-capable Role

## Steps

1. Instead of removing the Member, attempt to edit their Role(s) to remove the manage-capable Role entirely (leaving them with only a non-managing Role)

## Expected result

This action is blocked with the same class of error as direct removal, since it would leave the Project without a manage-capable Member, violating [[BR-PRJ-04]].

## Notes

verifies the protection can't be routed around via role-edit instead of removal
