## Preconditions

- The affected Member has an active browser session open
- Their Role is changed from 'Viewer' to 'Team Member'

## Steps

1. With the Member's session still open, have them attempt to create a Work Package (previously disallowed as Viewer)

## Expected result

On their next action/reload, the Member can now create a Work Package, reflecting the updated permission immediately.
