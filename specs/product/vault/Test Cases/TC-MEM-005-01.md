## Preconditions

- A Member with the Member Role exists on the public Demo project

## Steps

1. Click Remove next to the Member
2. Confirm

## Expected result

The Member no longer appears in the Members list. On their next page load they can still view the Project, as any logged-in non-member can, but can no longer create Work Packages: the Create button on the Work Packages list is disabled.
