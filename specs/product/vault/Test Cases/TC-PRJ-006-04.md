## Preconditions

- Project has existing Work Packages, Boards, and Members before archiving

## Steps

1. Archive the Project
2. Un-filter/access the archived Project directly
3. Inspect its Work Packages, Boards, and Members

## Expected result

All previously existing Work Packages, Boards, and Members remain intact and viewable.
