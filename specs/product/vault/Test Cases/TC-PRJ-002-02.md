## Preconditions

- A Project exists with a saved Identifier

## Steps

1. Open the Project's 'Change identifier' form
2. Enter an Identifier containing upper case letters, spaces or special characters (e.g. 'My Project!')
3. Click Update

## Expected result

The change is rejected with a validation error; the Project keeps its previous Identifier. The form states the permitted form — 1 to 100 characters, lower case letters, numbers, dashes and underscores only.
