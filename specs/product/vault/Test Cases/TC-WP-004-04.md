## Preconditions

![[TC-WP-004-03#^a-blocks-b]]

## Steps

1. Open Work Package B and open the Status selector
2. Close Work Package A
3. Reload B and open the Status selector again

## Expected result

While A is open, no closed Status is offered for B and no override control exists. After A is closed, 'Closed' is offered for B.
