## Preconditions

![[TC-WP-013-01#^open-child]]
- C has a child Work Package D that is Closed

## Steps

1. Open Work Package A and choose Delete from its menu
2. Tick the acknowledgement that all descendants will be removed
3. Confirm the deletion

## Expected result

A, C and D are all deleted — D too, although it is Closed. None of them appears in the Work Package list, opening any of them shows that it does not exist, and nothing offers to restore them.
